James LaplumeQuébec, Canada

Build to understand.

I focus on complete systems: what connects them, what makes them fragile, and what makes them explainable.

Profile

A systems builder, not a seller of promises.

My work combines systems thinking, networking, automation, documentation, and hands-on experimentation. I am building the Ambulance Lab to test these disciplines in an environment that does not tolerate vague architecture.

JARVIS Twin is the logical next step: a product in development designed to preserve a system’s memory, detect anomalies, and explain probable causes.

Principles

01

Facts before hypotheses

Always separate what is observed from what is assumed.

02

Local-first

Keep critical functions close to the environment.

03

Safety and reversibility

Limit permissions and plan a rollback.

04

Real documentation

A system must remain understandable after it is built.

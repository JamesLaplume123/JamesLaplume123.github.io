The studio

Build systems people can understand.

James Laplume is developing an independent studio at the intersection of automation, local intelligence and connected environments.

A field-first approach.

The work starts by observing what actually happens: requests lost between tools, documents nobody can find, duplicate entry and connected systems that do not communicate.

The solution is built small, tested with real data and documented before it expands.

01 // CLARITY

Show what is real.

Prototypes, experiments and work in progress are labelled honestly.

02 // CONTROL

Keep people in the loop.

Sensitive systems request approval, retain manual control and log their actions.

03 // INDEPENDENCE

Avoid closed cages.

Standards, APIs and local control let your system evolve without starting over.

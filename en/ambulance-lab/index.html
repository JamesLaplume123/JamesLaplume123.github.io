Ambulance LabFord 2017

JARVIS first real laboratory.

A mobile unit where energy, networking, security, and automation must work together — locally and under real conditions.

StatusActive buildProject visualization · evidence published progressively

The mission

Build an autonomous environment, then learn from every system.

The ambulance is not the product. It is the test bed that forces JARVIS Twin to deal with real dependencies, real constraints, and reproducible failures.

Seven systems, one architecture

Explore the laboratory.

Each domain produces useful data. JARVIS Twin must learn their relationships, not simply display them.

Target architecture

Local by default. Connected by choice.

The local network, Home Assistant, and controllers continue to function without depending on the cloud. External access remains limited and revocable.

Ambulance Lab // 2017 Ford V10

The laboratory where everything has to work for real.

Energy, networking, security, climate and automation brought together in a former Quebec ambulance.

Active buildReal dataLocal control

A test bench before a showcase.

The project carries the constraints of a small intelligent building inside the space, energy budget and connectivity of a vehicle.

Each integration is documented, tested locally and designed to keep working when the Internet disappears.

On-board systems

Seven layers. One environment.

Active layer

Energy

Two 280 Ah LiFePO₄ batteries are instrumented and monitored read-only.

01 // ENERGY

Measure before automating.

Batteries, protections and loads are observed in real time. Public telemetry remains strictly read-only.

02 // NETWORK

A private system on the move.

The local server, devices and interfaces stay reachable through VPN without exposing controls publicly.

03 // AUTOMATION

Useful scenes, with manual control.

Home Assistant coordinates state and automation. JARVIS adds context, explanation and supervision.

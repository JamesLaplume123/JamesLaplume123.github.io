English overview

A clear public map of an ambitious research and product vision.

The project is organized into six solution families: smart environments, private AI and knowledge, intelligent security, business automation, network resilience, and diagnostics & continuity. The detailed multi-page edition is currently maintained in French.

01

Smart environments

Connect comfort, energy, access and devices without making the environment harder to use.

02

Private AI and knowledge

Work with authorized documents and data while keeping provenance and permissions visible.

03

Intelligent security

Turn cameras, access and alarms into understandable events and organized evidence.

04

Operations automation

Move requests, documents and approvals between the tools a business already uses.

05

Network resilience

Understand infrastructure, dependencies, private access and service degradation.

06

Diagnostics & continuity

Move from a symptom to evidence, verification steps and a documented recovery.

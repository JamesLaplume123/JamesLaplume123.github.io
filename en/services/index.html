The vision // In plain language

One system that understands
what should work together.

I am building JARVIS to connect information, software and equipment people already use. The goal is simple: fewer disconnected systems, less wasted time and more control.

Important

JARVIS is under construction. Some abilities are being demonstrated, others are tested in the ambulance and several remain on the roadmap. This page shows the real direction without pretending everything is already available.

01 // Digital work

Move work forward without copying it everywhere.

This covers office tasks, field teams and internal knowledge. The point is not to sell “AI.” It is to create a simpler way to work.

01.1Development priority

Operations automation

Simply put: a request moves automatically from one step to the next.

Example: an email creates the file, assigns work, requests approval and prepares follow-up.

Under the hood: Automation · Mail Agent · Connectors · Evidence
01.2After Automation

Field team applications

Simply put: employees capture the right information in the right place from the field.

Example: inspections, photos, hours, inventory, signatures and reports.

Under the hood: App · Automation · Connectors
01.3Development priority

Private AI and document assistant

Simply put: ask questions about your own documents and see where the answer came from.

Example: find a procedure, summarize a contract or explain a file.

Under the hood: Knowledge · Documents Agent · Identity / Permissions
01.4Validate for each use

Agents that can act

Simply put: the assistant may prepare or trigger an approved action, not just answer.

Example: create a task, draft email, start a process or request approval.

Under the hood: Agents · Automation · Policy · Audit

The difference: automation moves the work, the application serves the field employee and the assistant helps people understand information. JARVIS connects all three.

02 // Places and equipment

Give the physical world one shared logic.

A home, RV, workshop or building already has many systems. JARVIS aims to make them work together while keeping local controls.

02.1Lab / future pilots

Intelligent home and building

Simply put: lighting, climate, doors, pumps and sensors work together.

Example: a scene adapts the space to presence, time and real needs.

Under the hood: Control · I/O · Home Assistant / KNX · Automation
02.2Main laboratory

Intelligent RV and ambulance

Simply put: bring the same intelligence to a vehicle that must work without Internet.

Example: water, lighting, climate, doors, batteries, mobile network and alarms.

Under the hood: JARVIS RV · I/O · Control · Energy · Diagnostics
02.3Lab → future offer

Intelligent energy

Simply put: see where energy goes, detect anomalies and protect autonomy.

Example: batteries, solar, generator, consumption, alerts and load priorities.

Under the hood: Energy · Control · Diagnostics
02.4Roadmap · privacy framework required

Vision and physical security

Simply put: understand camera, door and alarm events together.

Example: connect an open door, motion and an image in the same incident.

Under the hood: Vision · Physical Security · Event Layer

03 // Network, diagnostics and security

See what is happening before trying to fix it.

These abilities require more training, procedures and responsibility, so they are clearly separated from what will be developed first.

03.1After network training

Network visibility

Simply put: know what is connected, what changed and what is not working.

Example: new devices, slow network, connection loss and simple topology.

Under the hood: Network / Sentinel
03.2Specialized service later

Architecture and hardening

Simply put: organize the network and limit unnecessary access.

Example: separated IoT devices, VPN, access rules and clear documentation.

Under the hood: Network / Sentinel · Professional Services
03.3Authorized work later

OSINT and digital investigation

Simply put: research and document public information within an authorized mandate.

Example: companies, public assets, digital presence and open sources.

Under the hood: JARVIS Pro · Research · Evidence
03.4After skills and insurance

Pentest and cyber audit

Simply put: test an authorized environment and clearly explain weaknesses.

Example: exposure, configuration, vulnerabilities and recommendations.

Under the hood: Sentinel · JARVIS Pro · Professional Services
03.5Lab → future offer

Intelligent diagnostics

Simply put: combine symptoms, history and manuals to find faults faster.

Example: slow Internet, offline camera, abnormal battery or failed pump.

Under the hood: Diagnostics · Site Graph · Knowledge · Evidence
03.6Future recurring service

JARVIS Care

Simply put: watch system health after installation and act before everything breaks.

Example: backups, updates, errors, recovery and authorized remote support.

Under the hood: Care · Telemetry · Evidence · Connectors

Public method

Complete results, even when they are bad.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Public Kraken data, timestamped and checked for gaps and freshness.

  2. 02

    Decide

    A deterministic signal separated from the final decision and its rejections.

  3. 03

    Simulate

    Paper buys and sells with fees and slippage applied on both sides.

  4. 04

    Audit

    Net P&L, drawdown, history, assumptions, and strategy version retained.

Security boundary

The site can observe. It cannot trade.

The home server pushes a filtered, signed copy to the site. No inbound port, Home Assistant command, or broker key is exposed.
